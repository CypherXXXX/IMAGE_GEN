STYLE PREFIX FOR PROMPTS: [user fills, prepended to every prompt below at generation time]

Rules for any style, as they apply to this video:

- Milo and every other figure is an adult with adult stick-figure proportions: small head, long thin limbs. Every character who matters to a shot has a simple readable face (eyes, brows, a mouth line) that changes expression from shot to shot. Nobody is faceless.
- No lip-sync anywhere. Delivery is carried by posture, gesture and a held expression; the mouth never runs a speech cycle.
- No text, letters, numbers, labels, logos or interface elements inside any image or animation. Every word on screen is a TXT card added in the editor, including the phone message, the folder label, the sticky note and every diagram label.
- Nothing graphic. The toy snake, the spider and the toy crocodile are simple low-detail shapes. No gore, no wounds, no realistic reptile or insect detail.
- Not children's content in any style: no oversized round eyes, no cute rounded bodies, no pastel palette, no toy-like props.
- Lighting comes from what the scene is. The dusk path and the night kitchen are genuinely dark with one source; the kitchen by day, the meeting rooms, the lab and the corridor are flat and ordinary. Diagrams sit on a plain pale background regardless of the scene around them.
- Colour only where it carries meaning: the accent colour on a diagram, the glow of the phone screen, the aged wash on the left-hand list column. Never a decorative palette.
